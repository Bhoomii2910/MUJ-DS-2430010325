## Capstone in one paragraph

Predicts next-year % of a country's population using safely managed drinking water, from the previous 5 years of water access, urban population and unemployment (158 countries, 2000-2024). Linear Regression, Random Forest and a small Transformer are compared on a time-based split. See [`capstone/README.md`](capstone/README.md) for results and the installation guide.
